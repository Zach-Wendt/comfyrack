## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-01

### Added

- First public release.
- A catalog of ComfyUI workflows that agents and apps find, check and run by name, with `--json` output.
- `setup <workflow>` lists the node packs and models a machine is missing; `--apply` installs them through
  ComfyUI-Manager.
- `share` makes a ComfyUI reachable from your other machines over Tailscale.
- A demo and measured times for a 12 GB and a 32 GB card in the README.
